      A page to practise on

      PaneMux lets you use the web from the keyboard. Keys do nothing strange while you're typing in a box — the moment you click into one, PaneMux steps aside until you press Esc.

      This article is long on purpose, so there's something to scroll. Keep going with j.

      A link Another link Open the next lesson One more

      Scrolling works on any page: j down, k up, h and l sideways, d and u for half a screen, gg to the top, G to the bottom.

      Link hints (f) put letters on everything clickable. Type the letters and PaneMux clicks it for you. F opens the link in a new tab instead.

      Search the page with /, then n and N to hop between matches.

      Go back and forward with H and L. Switch tabs with J and K.

      And whenever you forget, ? shows every key.
